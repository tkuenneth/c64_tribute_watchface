### Welcome to C64 Tribute Watchface

<img src="./artwork/app_symbol.png" width="10%">

This watchface for WearOS by Google depicts the legendary colors of the Commodore 64 as well as its iconic font. Based on system settings time is shown in 12 or 24 hour format.

C64 Tribute Watchface is published under the terms of the [GNU General Public License Version 3](./gpl-3.0.txt). It uses the Pet Me 64 font by Rebecca G. Bettencourt, Kreative Software, in accordance with the Kreative Software Relay Fonts Free Use License in [license.txt](./license.txt). The first version of this app (well, actually, its source code) appeared 2015 in the third edition of my book about Android development called *Android 5*.

Get it for free on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thomaskuenneth.c64watchface).
