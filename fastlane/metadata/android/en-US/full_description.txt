C64 Tribute Watch Face shows the time in the colors and the character set of the Commodore 64: light blue on blue, inside the border that framed the screen in 1982.

The time follows your watch's 12 or 24 hour setting. Three options decide what else appears:

- seconds
- date, as the short weekday and the day of the month
- uppercase only, the way the C64 printed text by default

Under the last line sits a blinking block cursor. It blinks at the rate the Commodore 64 used: its KERNAL reloaded the counter with 20 and the timer ran at about 60 Hz, which is one phase every 333 milliseconds.

In ambient mode the face turns white on black and the cursor stops, so the time stays readable while few pixels are lit.

Round, square and rectangular watches each get a layout of their own.

- no ads
- no tracking
- no data collected
- open source
